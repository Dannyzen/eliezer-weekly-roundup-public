# Daily Agent Research Roundup: 2026-11-01

## Scope

There is no Sunday arXiv listing. This scan uses four non-duplicate papers from the Friday, October 30 listing, submitted October 28 or 29. External repositories were inspected read-only only.

## 1. Specialize evidence collection before aggregating a repair

ExploreRepair assigns distinct evidence dimensions to three agents and aggregates their source-linked trajectories and patches. Across 204 vulnerabilities, it reports material gains over prior repair systems under incomplete information.

Action now: use orthogonal evidence lanes, typed returns, and independent final verification. Do not pay for duplicate agents with the same prompt and context.

Implementability score: 0.72

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39899v1

## 2. Bind repair evaluation to the deployed executor

A repair that helps one model can hurt another because the remaining trajectory depends on who executes it. Greedy replay and stronger substitute models lose measurable deployment value on ALFWorld and WebShop.

Action now: fingerprint model, prompt, history protocol, tools, and runtime in every repair label, then validate with independent rollouts of the deployed policy.

Implementability score: 0.86

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38610v1

## 3. Treat repository instructions as an untrusted control surface

Malicious memory files changed secure-completion outcomes inconsistently, and agents could identify the attack while still producing vulnerable code. Instruction-file awareness is not a security boundary.

Action now: protect and separately review `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, and similar files, bind them to provenance, and prevent them from increasing runtime authority.

Implementability score: 0.90

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38521v1

## 4. Use pre-disclosure probes as warnings, not policy

Activation probes can identify a leaking call shortly before output, but a trained bag-of-words classifier comes close, permitted disclosures also trigger, and broad halting produces heavy benign disruption.

Action now: begin with a trained text baseline in shadow mode and keep deterministic recipient, purpose, and secret-release checks authoritative.

Implementability score: 0.38

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40588v1 and https://github.com/leonidas1712/precrime-agents

## Daily conclusion

The control plane needs two separations: evidence collection from decision authority, and behavioral warning from effect authorization. Specialize exploration, bind judgments to the exact executor, and keep context and model signals below typed runtime policy.
