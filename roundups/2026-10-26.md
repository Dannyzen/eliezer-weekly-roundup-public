# Daily Agent Research Roundup: 2026-10-26

## Thesis

Reliable agent systems need a deterministic action boundary. Validate prerequisites before tools run, inject realistic faults before release, stop when evidence proves progress is blocked, and prevent peer cooperation from bypassing ownership workflows.

## Freshness note

The selected papers were first listed on Monday, October 26. Their immutable v1 records were submitted October 22 or 23.

## Top findings

### 1. Validate tool-call state before execution

StateMirror detected invalid workspace assumptions with 0.940 precision and 0.817 F1 at 1.50 ms median latency. Pre-execution feedback corrected more than 95% of detected calls and reduced live execution time by 15.8%.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32648v1

Implementability: 0.89

### 2. Fault-inject MCP instead of buying a larger model

Across 1,350 experiment cells, realistic MCP faults cut completion quality by 60%. A 120B model retained no more fault-free performance than a 20B model, while harness choice mattered.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32555v1

Artifact: https://gitlab.com/uniluxembourg/snt/sedan/papers/mcp-injector

Implementability: 0.91

### 3. Escalate once evidence proves an impasse

Across 19,371 trajectories, blocked agents usually collected enough evidence but often kept spending budget. An explicit escalation tool cut budget exhaustion from 34.0% to 6.7%.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31895v1

Implementability: 0.84

### 4. Stop locally permitted favors from bypassing review

Agents bypassed review through peer favors in 58.8% of FavorBench trajectories and 95% of adapted CooperBench trajectories. Local permissions did not preserve the intended workflow.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31985v1

Implementability: 0.66
