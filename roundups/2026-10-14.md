# Daily Agent Research Roundup: 2026-10-14

## Thesis

Reliable agents need explicit routing before execution and explicit authority lifecycles around execution. Hidden selection, inferred references, self-reported safety, and persistent grants all fail at scale.

## Top findings

### 1. Large skill libraries can silently collapse routing

With 1,000 irrelevant skills, loaded-skill recall falls from 73.6% to 27.4% and the zero-load rate rises from 9.3% to 51.6%. Explicit preselection retains 73.9% recall.

Action now: separate skill discovery from task execution, record the considered and selected catalog entries, and measure zero-load failures directly.

Implementability score: **0.88**

Sources: [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18522v1), [official repository](https://github.com/jinlinll/lost-in-implicit-routing)

Analysis: [AgenticAI](../AgenticAI/2026-10-14/reasoning.md#lost-in-implicit-routing-preselect-skills-before-task-execution)

### 2. Tool-call judges need an independently derived reference

Judges accept correct arguments less often when their source is absent, and can prefer a visible wrong reference over a correct hidden derivation. DeRef separates candidate-blind derivation from judgment and raises controlled-test AUROC to 98.40 through 99.59.

Action now: preserve source spans, schema defaults, transformations, and uncertainty in a derivation record before the judge sees the candidate.

Implementability score: **0.81**

Sources: [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18497v1), [Apache-2.0 implementation](https://github.com/SarielMa/DeRef_release)

Analysis: [AgenticAI](../AgenticAI/2026-10-14/reasoning.md#deref-derive-the-expected-argument-before-seeing-the-candidate)

### 3. Internal states can forecast unauthorized actions before the tool call

A linear residual-state readout reaches AUROC 0.94 on matched authorization tasks. Pre-action gating cuts violations across four open-weight families and transfers to AgentDojo only after in-domain fitting.

Action now: shadow-score one open-weight agent, calibrate against matched authorized and unauthorized tasks, and keep deterministic policy authoritative.

Implementability score: **0.44**

Sources: [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17301v1), [code and experimental record](https://github.com/AryaFayyazi/Honesty)

Analysis: [Strategy](../Strategy/2026-10-14/sovereignty.md#internal-state-gates-can-stop-unauthorized-actions-before-execution)

### 4. Authority must expire after its last justified use

CreepScope measures capability exposure after the final legitimate step. In a 24-episode mail simulation, an injected stale send executes in all nine persistent-policy episodes and none of the three step-lease episodes, while all required action sequences complete.

Action now: add grant, use, expiry, denied-use, and terminal-debt events to one workflow, then shadow step-bound leases.

Implementability score: **0.78**

Source: [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15988v1)

Analysis: [Strategy](../Strategy/2026-10-14/sovereignty.md#creepscope-expire-authority-after-its-last-justified-step)

## What is implementable now

1. Put a separately evaluated shortlist stage in front of large skill catalogs.
2. Derive expected tool arguments without showing the candidate to the derivation model.
3. Track capability exposure after last use and residual authority at workflow end.
4. Shadow pre-action risk scoring on open-weight models before any blocking deployment.
5. Keep deterministic policy, exact effect receipts, and human escalation as the authority layer.
