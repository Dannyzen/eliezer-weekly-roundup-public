# Daily Agent Research Roundup: 2026-11-24

## Thesis

Production agent quality is a property of the model, harness, serving substrate, effect boundary, and recovery plane together. Today's strongest sources make those hidden layers measurable.

## Top findings

### 1. Count dropped work as failed quality

MASSys replays the same multi-agent candidates under different serving substrates and loads. At higher load, evaluated-only scores stayed relatively stable while effective quality over all submitted jobs collapsed.

Action now: report submitted, completed, execution-successful, evaluated, and correct jobs separately, then bind the result to runtime and load identity.

Implementability score: 0.62

Source: [The Evaluation Substrate Matters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28671v1)

### 2. Attack the grader before trusting the score

Hugging Face's agentic-evaluation guide demonstrates an eight-step maintenance loop. In its worked example, a shortcut passed 8 of 8 tasks until the verifier checked the required intermediate work.

Action now: test every verifier with a reference solution, no-op, and plausible shortcut before running expensive model comparisons.

Implementability score: 0.92

Source: [How to build and maintain an agentic evaluation](https://huggingface.co/blog/agentic-evals)

### 3. Enforce policy on effects, not tool-call descriptions

AgentBouncer mediates system calls before execution. Across 340 attacks, reported attack success fell from 99.41% to 10.59% while benign task success remained comparable.

Action now: add host-level pre-effect checks for file deletion, process launch, network access, credentials, and package mutation. Preserve deterministic denials and receipts.

Implementability score: 0.48

Source: [AgentBouncer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30621v1)

### 4. Keep backups and credentials outside the agent's authority

A preregistered study across database engines found that vendor minimum-backup configurations recovered every protected-row loss in its model-free relational tests. Least privilege still allowed destructive data writes, and agents used a planted superuser credential in 24 of 4,032 least-privilege trials.

Action now: combine scoped identities, independent deny rules, credential separation, and exercised restores. Verify final state independently of the agent's report.

Implementability score: 0.86

Source: [When an AI agent makes a mistake in a database, what decides the damage?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30483v1)

## What changed in the stack model

1. Evaluation quality includes the serving substrate and submitted-job denominator.
2. Verifiers need adversarial tests and versioned evidence.
3. Tool authority must terminate at the actual effect boundary.
4. Recovery and administrator credentials must remain outside the acting identity.

## Evidence boundary

The arXiv sources were submitted on 21 or 23 November and first listed on 24 November in cs.MA or cs.SE. The Hugging Face guide was published on 23 November. External artifacts were inspected read-only. No external source code was cloned or executed.
