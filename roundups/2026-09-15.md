# Daily Agent Research Roundup: 2026-09-15

## Thesis

The runtime must control two scarce things: evidence and authority. Search more evidence only when a long trace leaves meaningful artifacts unread; spend on collaboration only when difficulty warrants it; bind tools to exact manifests; and do not accept a successful call without effect semantics.

## Top findings

### 1. Pin MCP identity before trust

A census of 21,643 official-registry servers finds that 40.58 percent of multi-version servers changed meaning or destination under stable identifiers and 4.16 percent redirected endpoint hosts. Tool definitions are not versioned by the registry. Pin package, source, schema, transport, and origin in one approval manifest.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14119v1
Implementability: 0.88

### 2. Make long-trace diagnosis search unread evidence

Continual Search raises Opus-4.8 F1 from 0.478 to 0.620 on 50 MegaRCA-Mix failures with 286K-token median records by expanding evidence coverage from 70.8 to 97.4 percent. It does not reliably help short traces, so gate it by unread evidence and coverage gain.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13463v1
Implementability: 0.82

### 3. Successful tool calls still need transaction semantics

Eight external-effect anomalies show how retries, speculation, concurrency, and partial failure can leave duplicate, missing, provisional, or aborted effects. Standard MCP hints fully express none of the required status, idempotency, compensation, staging, dependency, coordination, or visibility contracts.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15397v1
Artifact: https://github.com/flame-stream/mcp-annotation-census
Implementability: 0.78

### 4. Route collaboration by difficulty at equal spend

Hierarchical collaboration costs about 9.95 times more tokens than a single call. Its gain rises from 2.4 pass@1 points on easy problems to 21.1 on hard ones. The practical control is per-task topology routing with every candidate calibrated to the same realized budget.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13890v1
Implementability: 0.76
