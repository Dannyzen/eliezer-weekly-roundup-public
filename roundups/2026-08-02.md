## Top findings

### 1. PAIChecker makes benchmark admission a checked contract

It finds 13.6 percent PR-issue misalignment in SWE-bench Verified and reaches up to 92.12 percent and 91.67 percent binary accuracy on two other coding-agent datasets.

Action now: bind issue, PR, base revision, patch, tests, discussions, labels, and adjudication before a task enters evaluation or training.

Implementability score: **0.84**

Sources:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28587v1
- https://github.com/manyifire/PAIChecker

### 2. Local CUAs benefit from compact history, not unlimited compute

One recent screenshot raises average success from about 18 percent to more than 25 percent. History length four is best in the reported sweep; longer histories, more steps, planner-grounder splits, and parallel plans add cost or convert stalls into false success.

Action now: add compact state history, no-progress detection, deterministic completion checks, and measured escalation.

Implementability score: **0.76**

Source:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28573v1

### 3. Audio perception is an authority boundary

AudioAgentSecurity tests eight scenarios, ten attack patterns, and eleven multimodal agents. It reports 69.10 percent average attack success against Gemini 3 Pro, while a source-separation and consistency defense reports more than 90 percent detection across diverse attacks.

Action now: source-label audio, quarantine overlaps, and require trusted-channel confirmation before consequential tools act.

Implementability score: **0.64**

Sources:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28165v1
- https://github.com/Limax666/AudioAgentSecurity
- https://huggingface.co/datasets/Limax11/AudioAgentSecurity
