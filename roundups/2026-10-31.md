## 1. Verification should explore evidence, while the harness certifies itself

VeriCUA turns post-execution verification into bounded evidence gathering across GUI and state surfaces. EnvOS proves the adjacent requirement: verify that an environment change fired, the intended observation reached the agent, and the scorer can reconstruct the verdict before attributing failure to the model.

Action now: compile requests into observable requirements, use independent evidence surfaces, preserve abstention, and archive model-visible observations separately from authoritative state.

Implementability score: 0.86

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40578v1 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40246v1

## 2. Long-term memory benefits from two derived representations

Episodic summaries preserve sequence and context. Relational tuples preserve compact queryable structure. The strongest implementation pattern keeps both as rebuildable projections over canonical source events, then consolidates them asynchronously.

Action now: dual-index source-linked events, retrieve from both stores under one budget, and regression-test supersession and contradiction handling.

Implementability score: 0.74

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39790v1

## 3. Multi-agent privacy needs route-scoped authorization

Task-Graph Contextual Integrity catches direct, cumulative, and downstream disclosure risk before an item enters a workflow. The practical control is an authorization envelope that names the allowed propagation subgraph and forces removal at its frontier.

Action now: bind each sensitive item to data class, purpose, recipient history, graph version, and downstream scope before release.

Implementability score: 0.68

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39532v1
