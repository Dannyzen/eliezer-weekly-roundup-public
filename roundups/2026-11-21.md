# Daily Agent Research: 2026-11-21

## Thesis

The harness is becoming both an engineering product and a safety boundary. Improve it through traceable requirements, evaluate it in its deployed loop, and refuse action when mandate or state is incomplete.

## Top findings

### 1. Requirement-centered harness evolution

AHE-Beta turns user trajectory evidence into reviewed harness requirements and reports 33.3% pass@1 versus 21.9% for its base harness on 38 held-out tasks, with 40.8% fewer tokens.

Implement now: evidence-backed requirement issues, root-cause consolidation, requirement-specific merge proofs, and separate held-out evaluation.

Implementability score: 0.65

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24924v1 and https://github.com/Curry09/AHE-Beta

### 2. Harness-specific RL adapters

Hugging Face trained Qwen3.8-27B adapters inside pi, Hermes Agent, and OpenCode. The pi result moved Terminal-Bench 2.1 from 39% to 55% while reducing average tokens from 2.9K to 1.5K, but out-of-distribution transfer was mixed.

Implement now: exact-harness replay, verifier controls, adapter bakeoffs, and separate transfer metrics. Full RL training remains infrastructure-heavy.

Implementability score: 0.45

Sources: https://huggingface.co/blog/harness-adapters-at-scale and https://huggingface.co/collections/harness-hub/qwen38-27b-harness-adapters

### 3. Continuation audits for overeagerness

Continuation Audits grounds safety tests in real coding-session prefixes. Across 900 audits, evaluation awareness did not exceed unaudited sessions, and the three tested models showed different authority-failure surfaces.

Implement now: redacted trajectory prefixes, disposable fixtures, single-behavior seeds, and separate deviation/consequence scores.

Implementability score: 0.60

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24491v1

### 4. Answerability audits before model judgment

Paired-state tests ask whether the model received enough operational state to decide safely. In 15 paired cases, text and rules alone were insufficient; adding state improved three of four models.

Implement now: paired opposite-outcome cases, state-freshness checks, fail-closed missing-state handling, and paired correctness metrics.

Implementability score: 0.90

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24682v1

## What to do now

1. Admit harness changes through evidence-backed requirements.
2. Evaluate the exact deployed harness, model, tools, and verifier together.
3. Add known-wrong and valid-alternative controls to task validators.
4. Test mandate overreach from realistic trajectory prefixes.
5. Require fresh state and policy snapshots before action release.
