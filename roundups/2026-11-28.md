# Daily Agent Research: 2026-11-28

## Thesis

Reliable agents need source-aware context and cost-aware orchestration. Authority must survive contact with writable workspaces, and multi-agent gains must survive equal-budget comparisons.

## Top findings

### 1. Authority-aware workspace context

Pydantic AI v2.77.0 keeps low-authority repository context out of the instruction channel. Skills, memory, and repository files arrive as tool output or attributed data instead of application-authored instructions.

Implement now: source authority on context files, instruction-channel admission, tool-mediated low-authority reads, and planted-instruction probes.

Implementability score: 0.93

Sources: https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/releases/tag/v2.77.0 and https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/11837

### 2. Attack-policy envelopes for monitor evaluation

A fixed GPT-5.4 monitor's false-negative rate ranged from 0.5% to 20.8% across reinforcement-learning optimization levels and from 6.4% to 23.0% across attack methods at their best observed levels.

Implement now: version attackers, sweep optimization levels, hold out the target monitor, and report the full miss-rate envelope.

Implementability score: 0.78

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34934v1

### 3. Cost-matched subagent delegation

On random repository-construction tasks, parallel subagents improved hidden-test pass share by about 1 to 5 percentage points at 2.6 to 3.8 times the cost. Forced self-checking recovered much of the gain cheaply in one model setting.

Implement now: single-agent, forced-self-check, fresh-session, and subagent controls under equal cost and time.

Implementability score: 0.86

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33893v1

### 4. Trainable swarm communication

A reproducible four-agent TRL setup trained one shared Qwen3-0.6B policy to pool private evidence. Training success rose from 6.6% to 82.3% while generated tokens per training attempt fell 37.4%, though held-out token efficiency was not retained.

Implement now: verifiable shared-information tasks, communication traces, matched-budget baselines, and shared-policy training. The GPU requirement keeps this architecture-heavy.

Implementability score: 0.45

Sources: https://huggingface.co/blog/training-swarms, https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08615v1, and https://github.com/huggingface/trl

## What to do now

1. Demote writable workspace text from instructions to attributed data.
2. Report monitor quality across attacker methods and optimization budgets.
3. Require a forced-self-check baseline before paying for subagents.
4. Score multi-agent systems on success, tokens, duplicate work, and integration cost together.
