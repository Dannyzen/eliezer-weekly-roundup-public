# Daily Synthesis: 2026-10-18

## Thesis

Today's findings move agent quality and safety toward evidence-bearing runtime objects: customer outcomes for delivery, experiment windows for learning, and sandbox state for execution authority.

## Top findings

### 1. Agent evaluation should grade customer uplift

FDE-Gym tests 95 delivery tasks from 20 customer scenarios across 18 sectors. The best configuration averaged 0.56 and resolved 14 of 95 realistic tasks. Even perfect key-fact discovery was insufficient on most affected tasks, which shows that clarification and delivery quality need separate evidence.

Sources: [FDE-Gym paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22350v1), [repository](https://github.com/deeplethe/fde-gym), [open examples](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DeepLethe/FDE-Gym-examples)

Implementability score: 0.82

### 2. Long-horizon training needs execution windows

MLExpert groups the decisions between experiment results into one reward window and filters infrastructure failures from model-error learning. The paper reports a 37.9% to 72.7% medal-rate increase on 22 MLE-Bench Lite competitions, while the public model and task factory expose a substantial but infrastructure-heavy implementation path.

Sources: [MLExpert paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22344v1), [repository](https://github.com/InternScience/MLExpert), [model](https://huggingface.co/InternScience/MLExpert)

Implementability score: 0.47

### 3. Sandbox governance needs a control plane outside the guest

SPACE exposes Firecracker sandboxes through explicit template, lifecycle-operation, process, command-session, and egress-policy objects. The strongest reusable pattern is to bind exact template identity and network authority before the guest runs, then preserve asynchronous operation and recovery receipts.

Sources: [official announcement](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/open-sourcing-space), [repository](https://github.com/perplexityai/SPACE), [architecture guide](https://space.docs.perplexity.ai/guide/architecture.html)

Implementability score: 0.64

## What is implementable now

1. Turn one real workflow into a hidden-acceptance case graded on operational outcome.
2. Record experiment windows with source revision, environment identity, cost, metrics, and failure class.
3. Bind sandbox creates to exact templates, principals, and egress profiles.
4. Add deterministic receipts for stakeholder contact, experiment results, sandbox operations, and recovery.

Highest score: 0.82 for outcome-based delivery evaluation with the public FDE-Gym harness and open examples.

Lowest score: 0.47 for MLExpert-style post-training. The execution-window methodology is usable now, while reproducing the 35B model training path requires substantial compute and evaluation infrastructure.
