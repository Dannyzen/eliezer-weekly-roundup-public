## Deep Dive Wednesday

The social harness won the week because it names the missing boundary between inter-agent transport and each principal's local execution authority. Across 600 released scheduling runs, capable agents and basic messaging did not reliably produce satisfactory cross-principal coordination. The durable architecture is a five-layer control plane for identity, ordered envelopes, personal firewalls, task-specific norms, and post-hoc accountability.

What is implementable now: one bounded workflow with signed message envelopes, a deterministic state machine, local effect gates, append-only receipts, and the released traces as regression fixtures. What remains conceptual: general norms across task families and the institutions that investigate and impose consequences.

## Top findings

### 1. Coding-agent ranks have outrun benchmark resolution

An audit of 254 SWE-bench submissions finds the leading two Verified entries tied at 396 of 500, with all 29 examined frontier pairs statistically unresolved. Use evidence-backed tiers, then break ties with cost, latency, recovery, repository fit, and scaffold identity.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17394v1
Artifact: https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/resolution-audit
Implementability: 0.86

### 2. Skill popularity and scanner consensus do not establish safety

The OpenClaw skill registry grew from 33,399 to 65,175 listings in 91 days. Of readable skills, 85.06 percent carried privilege evidence, 77.86 percent had no stars or comments, and three scanners disagreed on 23,702 of 61,990 shared listings. Admission needs provenance, privilege, review, probes, and host policy.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17274v1
Registry: https://github.com/openclaw/clawhub
Implementability: 0.84

### 3. Multi-principal agents need a social harness, not only transport

Across 600 released scheduling runs, honest agents degraded under scale and concurrency, while faulty agents could stall or steer outcomes. A practical social harness binds identity, ordered communication, task norms, action guardrails, and accountability above the transport layer.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17527v1
Artifact: https://github.com/social-harness/social-harness-paper
Implementability: 0.68

### 4. Human-visible UI is not the agent's observation

Across 546 mobile tasks, 13 apps, five frameworks, and three models, UI-desynchronization attacks produced 77.9 percent static and 66.9 percent dynamic misleading rates. Consequential actions need observation parity between screenshots, accessibility data, and the user's approval surface.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16732v1
Implementability: 0.62
